Lighting desk app

B-Box Lighting Desk

Run your rig from your computer. Program looks, lay fixtures out on a plan, record cues, and send it all out over Art-Net.

  • Command line: 1 thru 10 @ 50, colour red, record cue 1, go
  • Faders for every channel, with Release all
  • Patch, cues, effects and output pages
  • Art-Net output with a live packet counter
  • Grand master, blackout, and Back and GO buttons
Pixel mapping app

B-Box Pixel Mapper

LED pixel mapping for Windows. Build stages from your LED strips, mix effects live, record sequences and send it all to your lights over Art-Net or sACN.

  • Stages from LED strips, with RGB, RGBW and more LED types
  • Live mixing with A/B crossfade and effect layers
  • Record and play back sequences
  • One licence key activates on up to 3 computers
Android app

B-Box Android

Run your DMX from an Android phone or tablet. Connect over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi to Art-Net and sACN adaptors.

  • Cues with Back and Go buttons
  • LED tape modes: single, RGB, RGBW, RGBWW and bi-colour
  • Fixture control and a test screen
  • Try it free on channels 1–24
Free tool

DMX address calculator

Patch your rig in seconds. Build a library of your fixtures, tap one to put it on the next free address, and keep rigs, notes and photos for every job — all saved on your own device.

  • Patch lists across universes, never splitting a fixture
  • Saved fixtures and rigs, with photos and notes
  • PDF export of your patch, saved list and photos
  • Works offline, with backup and restore
