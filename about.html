About us

A UK team building for the people on set.

B-Box designs, makes and sells light controllers for film and television. Our wired and wireless DMX and CRMX units, lighting desk app and free tools like the DMX calculator exist for one reason: so gaffers, electricians and lighting designers spend less time fighting their kit and more time shaping light.

Talk to the team

  • Built for set days

    Quick to rig, easy to read in the dark, dependable from first call to wrap.

  • Wired, wireless, CRMX

    One family of controllers, so you can mix cable and wireless on the same job.

  • Support you can reach

    Questions go to the people who build the kit, not a call centre.
